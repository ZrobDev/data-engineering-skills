## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added
- Skills: `sql-tuning`, `data-cleaning`, `etl-pipelines`, `data-modeling`, `lakehouse`, `de-vendors`, `de-orchestrate`.
- Agents: `de-scout` (haiku), `de-builder` (sonnet), `de-reviewer` (sonnet).
- `de-vendors` puller with 9 pinned vendor repos, a scan before install, and a per-project lock.
- CI: content lint, structure validation, guard tests, Cisco skill-scanner, gitleaks, zizmor.
